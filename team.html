The people behind Trac

A master wigmaker who knows what good hair is worth, and the developer who built the app that proves where it comes from.

Founder

Merria Dearman

Founder, Provenir

A master wigmaker with more than two decades of experience, Merria founded Provenir, an atelier known for hyperrealistic, handcrafted wigs and toppers, especially for clients with medical hair loss.

Trac carries her standard back to the source: hair that is sourced humanely and processed responsibly.

Product & engineering

Teddy Aaron

Product Architect & Lead Developer, Trac

Teddy built Trac from the ground up, turning Provenir's idea into a platform for sourcing human hair ethically and traceably. He designed and developed the iOS app, its on-device AI checks and this website.

He also leads digital strategy at Provenir, including its SEO, website and online presence.
