The hair industry can't prove where its hair comes from.

Trac can.

Most hair passes through collectors and brokers before anyone knows who it came from or whether they agreed. Trac replaces that chain with one direct, recorded step.

How it works

From ponytail to payout in four steps

  1. 1

    Submit a lot

    Take four guided photos, confirm the details and complete a quick donor check. Trac shows a likely price range before you send anything.

  2. 2

    Get an offer

    A real person on the Trac team reviews your lot and sends a firm price, usually within one business day. Accept it or turn it down.

  3. 3

    Ship it free

    Accept and we send a prepaid, tracked label. Follow your parcel in the app from pickup to delivery.

  4. 4

    Get paid

    We inspect the hair against your submission. Once it passes, our payments team calls you to arrange payment.

On-device AI

The checking happens on your phone, in seconds

Trac uses Apple's built-in image recognition plus its own measurement code. It runs privately on the iPhone, so grading needs no internet. The AI checks and flags. People make every decision.

Instant photo check

Every photo is checked for sharpness, lighting and framing. A bad shot is caught before you send it.

Traceability

Every ponytail has a record

Each lot gets a reference number and a history that follows it all the way through.

  1. 1Verified donor
  2. 2Photos checked

When your hair arrives, our team checks it against its record on 10 points, including length, colour, condition, weight and contamination.

Ethics, built in

Consent you can prove

Sourcing ethically means knowing the person behind the hair agreed to sell it. Trac makes that part of every submission, not an afterthought.

A real, live donor

A quick head-turn check proves a real person is in front of the camera, not a photo. It runs on the phone and is not an identity check.

Consent on record

Every donor agrees to the sale in the app. If the donor is under 18, a parent or guardian has to approve.

Proof of origin

The phone’s location is compared with where the seller says the hair came from, so the origin is more than just their word.

Private by default

Donor records stay with Trac's verification team. Buyers never see a donor's name or face.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

Who can sell hair on Trac?

Anyone 18 or older can create a seller account. If the donor is under 18, a parent or guardian has to approve the sale.

How much is my hair worth?

Trac shows a likely price range from your hair's length, weight and texture before you send anything. The Trac team then makes a firm offer, which you can accept or decline.

How do I get paid?

Once your hair arrives and passes inspection, the Trac payments team calls you on the phone number on your account to arrange payment.

Does shipping cost anything?

No. When you accept an offer, Trac sends you a prepaid, tracked shipping label.

What happens to the face check?

The live face check runs on your phone to confirm a real person is present. It is not an identity check. One donor photo is kept privately for the verification team and is never shown to buyers.
