The people behind Trac

A master wigmaker who knows what good hair is worth, and the developer who built the app to prove where it comes from.

Founder

Merria Dearman

Founder, Provenir

A wigmaker with more than two decades of experience, Merria founded Provenir, a transformative hair atelier known for hyperrealistic, handcrafted wigs and toppers, especially for clients with medical hair loss.

Trac grew out of her belief that hair should be sourced humanely and processed responsibly.

Product

Teddy Aaron

Product Architect & Lead Developer, Trac
Digital Strategy & Operations, Provenir

Teddy built Trac (formerly Affidamento) from the ground up, turning Provenir's concept into a platform for ethically sourcing and evaluating human hair. He designed the iOS app, its on-device AI checks and this website.

He also supports Provenir's SEO, website and online presence, working closely with Merria and the core team.
